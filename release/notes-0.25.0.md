## 0.25.0 (beta): damage and repair, cargo and storage, ramps

Needs **AeroCore 0.42.x** and NeoForge 21.1.252+ for Minecraft 1.21.1. Saves from 0.24 load with every part as new.

- Damage: parts wear as they are used and are damaged by crashes, attacks and explosions, fire and lava, water and
  corrosion; worn parts do less. Occupants can be hurt. Sneak and right-click a vehicle with an empty hand for its
  status; warning lamps on the dashboard. Server options in `damage`.
- Repair: the repair shop room mends parts with materials, time and power; past 80 % a part must be replaced by a newly
  made one. Field repair kit and tyre patch kit to get home.
- Cargo: bodies' boots, beds and holds carry items, weighed into the physics (the inventory key while riding opens
  them); storage box parts (roof box, bed box, 20 ft container); the cargo loader. Every item has a mass.
- Storage blocks: crate, steel locker, parts rack, shipping container; rooms and shops take materials from them.
- Road ramps (asphalt, concrete; 1:2, 1:4, 1:8); bench charts for gearboxes, transfer cases, axles and landing gear.

---

- 損壞：零件會隨使用磨損，也會被碰撞、攻擊與爆炸、火與岩漿、泡水與腐蝕弄壞，壞了表現就變差；乘員會受傷。空手潛行右鍵看載具狀態，儀表有警示燈。伺服器設定在 `damage`。
- 修理：修理廠用料件、工時與電把零件修好；超過 80 % 的零件要換一個新做的。急修工具組與補胎片讓你開得回去。
- 載貨：車身的後車廂、貨斗、貨艙可以放物品，重量算進物理（坐在車上按物品欄鍵打開）；儲物箱零件（車頂箱、貨斗箱、20 呎貨櫃）；裝卸台。每種物品都有質量。
- 儲物方塊：木箱、鋼製置物櫃、零件架、貨櫃；組裝室與各種工坊從裡面取料。
- 路面坡道（柏油、混凝土；1:2、1:4、1:8）；變速箱、分動箱、車軸、起落架的試算圖。
- 需要 AeroCore 0.42.x 與 NeoForge 21.1.252 以上（Minecraft 1.21.1）。0.24 的存檔照讀，零件都是全新的。
