## 0.24.0 (beta): first public release

Needs **AeroCore 0.41.x** and NeoForge 21.1.252+ for Minecraft 1.21.1.

- New-player tutorial: the Vehicle Engineering Handbook (given once on first join, or a book and a blank blueprint),
  39 advancements in a High Degree of Freedom Vehicles tab, a welcome in the chat, JEI pages.
- English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese.
- Everything up to here: part design, machining and CNC, power, petroleum, assembly rooms, driving and flying, airports,
  the refit shop, the paint booth, synthesized sound (see the CHANGELOG).
