## 0.26.0 (beta): heavy machinery

Needs **AeroCore 0.43.x** and NeoForge 21.1.252+ for Minecraft 1.21.1. Saves from 0.25 load unchanged.

- Machine parts to design: crawler tracks, roller drums, outriggers, hydraulic pumps and motors (travel drives, winches),
  work links (booms, sticks, loader arms, telescopic and lattice booms, masts, slewing rings, centre hinges) and work
  tools of 33 kinds (buckets, blades, rippers, hammers, grapples, forks, bodies, screeds, milling drums, cutterheads...),
  operator cabs (ROPS, FOPS) and engine hoods.
- Joints and hydraulics: booms rise, houses slew, loaders steer on their hinges, bodies tip; a machine can tip over.
- Work mode (N): the movement keys and the arrows are two joysticks (ISO or SAE); X, R and F for the other functions.
- The world is dug: soil and rock come away as bulk weighed at real densities and are poured back as loose fill; rollers
  compact, pavers pave, cold planers mill, pile hammers drive piles, tunnel boring machines line their tunnels.
- The heavy yard (open-air assembly), the asphalt and concrete plants, truck and crawler cranes with hooks, the tower crane.
- Looks laid out in Blender: booms, buckets, blades, tracks, drums and bodies drawn from their designs, the hydraulic
  rams moving with their joints, the tower crane's lattice mast, jib, trolley and cab; the test excavators, loader and
  rollers have cabs and engine hoods. Every machine part and tool redrawn in Blender, and more detail on gearboxes,
  transfer cases, ladder frames, hydrogen tanks, the turbofan's cowl, wings and seven machine blocks.

- 可設計的機械零件：履帶、壓路機鋼輪、支腿、液壓泵與馬達（行走、捲揚機）、作業連桿（動臂、斗桿、舉升臂、伸縮臂、桁架臂、門架、迴轉環、中央鉸接）與 33 種工作裝置（鏟斗、推土鏟、鬆土器、液壓錘、抓斗、貨叉、車斗、熨平板、刨刀鼓、刀盤……），以及機械駕駛室（ROPS、FOPS）與引擎蓋。
- 關節與液壓：動臂升降、上部迴轉、裝載機以鉸接轉向、車斗舉升；伸太遠會翻車。
- 作業模式（N）：移動鍵與方向鍵是兩支搖桿（ISO 或 SAE），其他功能用 X、R、F。
- 真的挖世界：土石變成散料照真實密度算重，倒回地面成為鬆土；壓路機壓實、鋪築機鋪路、冷刨機刨除、打樁機打樁、隧道鑽掘機組立環片。
- 露天的重機組裝場、瀝青與混凝土拌合廠、汽車式與履帶式起重機的吊鉤、塔式起重機。
- 外觀先在 Blender 擺過：動臂、鏟斗、推土鏟、履帶、鋼輪與車斗照設計畫出，液壓油缸跟著關節動；塔吊有桁架標準節、吊臂、小車與駕駛室；測試用的挖土機、裝載機與壓路機都有駕駛室和引擎蓋。所有機械零件與工作裝置都用 Blender 重畫過，變速箱、分動箱、梯形車架、氫氣瓶、渦扇短艙、機翼與七種機器方塊也加了細節。
- 需要 AeroCore 0.43.x 與 NeoForge 21.1.252 以上（Minecraft 1.21.1）。0.25 的存檔照讀。
