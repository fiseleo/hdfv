# High Degree of Freedom Vehicles

**Design every part. Make it on real machine tools. Build cars, trucks, aircraft, helicopters and boats.**

High Degree of Freedom Vehicles is a vehicle mod in the spirit of *Automation*: nothing comes ready-made. You design
each part yourself in a design screen, every number is yours, and the physics (from
[AeroCore](https://modrinth.com/mod/aerocore)) decides what it does. Then you make it in a workshop of real machine
tools and put the vehicle together in an assembly room.

> **Beta.** Saves are kept working between versions where possible, but the format may still change. Back up your world.

## Features

- **Design anything:** piston engines (petrol, diesel, turbocharged) from sixteen components, turbojets, turbofans,
  turboprops, turboshafts, ramjets, scramjets, pulsejets, rockets; wings, propellers and rotors from their airfoils;
  modular car bodies, fuselages and hulls; chassis, axles, landing gear, gearboxes and transfer cases. Every design has
  live checks, a 3D view and a test bench (a dynamometer, a polar, a test track).
- **Make it:** new ores and smelting, an alloy furnace, a foundry and forging press, standard components, lengthenable
  multiblock lathes, mills, planers, boring mills and drill presses, nine families of machining (grinding, gears, EDM,
  laser, waterjet, riveting...), a CNC up to 12 axes, welding. Every part follows its own route of real steps.
- **Power it:** treadles, steam engines, line shafts, generators, cables, batteries, and your own engines on stands.
- **Build it:** car factories, hangars and shipyards you build yourself; vehicles laid out, built by a crane and
  welders from your parts, and rolled out.
- **Fuel it:** oil fields, drilling rigs, pump jacks, a distillation column, FCC, reformer, cracker, polymers.
- **Drive and fly it:** manual and automatic gearboxes, four-wheel drive, dials and lights; flight controls, landing
  gear, airports with control towers and ATC; articulated trailers.
- **Make it yours:** a refit shop (shape, size, aero kits, rims, tuning, lamps) and a paint booth with your own stickers.
- **Keep it running:** parts wear and get damaged (crashes, attacks, fire, water, rust); a repair shop and field kits
  put them right, and a part past 80 % is replaced by a new one.
- **Load it:** boots, beds and holds carry cargo that weighs; roof boxes, bed boxes and containers you design;
  crates, lockers, parts racks and a cargo loader.
- **Hear it:** every sound is synthesized from the running engine or machine.

## Getting started

New players get the **Vehicle Engineering Handbook**: right-click it. It goes from the first ore to your first car,
aircraft and boat. The advancements screen has a tab that follows it, and every item's tooltip tells its tech stage and
where it is made. JEI (optional) shows every recipe and machining step.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.252 or newer
- [AeroCore](https://modrinth.com/mod/aerocore) 0.42.x
- Optional: JEI (recipes, information pages), Create (some materials by crushing, mixing, pressing)
- Install on both the client and the server.

- **組起來：** 自己蓋的汽車廠、機庫與造船廠；配置好載具，由天車與焊工用你的零件造出來，再開出廠。
- **加油：** 油田、鑽井架、抽油機、常壓蒸餾塔、FCC、重組、裂解、聚合。

- **聽得到：** 所有聲音都依運轉中的引擎或機器即時合成。

新玩家會拿到《載具工程手冊》：右鍵打開，從第一塊礦石教到第一台車、飛機和船。

需求：Minecraft 1.21.1、NeoForge 21.1.252 以上、AeroCore 0.42.x；選用 JEI、Create；客戶端與伺服器都要裝。授權 LGPL-3.0-only。
