A paint booth ({item hdfv:paint_booth_controller}) repaints a finished vehicle: colours by part and pictures you upload as stickers, placed where you like. It uses {item hdfv:paint_can} and {item hdfv:sticker_film}, and takes time and electricity.

Stickers are shared with the other players on the server; operators can list and remove uploaded pictures (/hdfv decal).
