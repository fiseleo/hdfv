The manual machine tools are multiblocks you can lengthen. A lathe, seen from where you stand to work it:
- Bottom row: the drive at the left (a {item hdfv:pulley_drive} on the line shaft, or a {item hdfv:motor_drive} on cables), then one to six {item hdfv:lathe_bed} blocks: a metre of bed each.
- On top: the {item hdfv:lathe} block over the drive (the headstock); over the bed, optional accessories and a tailstock at the end.

{image lathe}

Use the lathe block: it checks the pattern and forms. A longer bed turns longer work; what does not fit, it says. The drill press and the planer are built the same way (JEI shows each machine's pattern and size).

{items hdfv:pulley_drive hdfv:lathe_bed hdfv:lathe hdfv:carbon_tool_set}

Cutting wears tools: put a {item hdfv:carbon_tool_set} in the consumable slot. A manual machine works only while someone works it: keep its screen open and stay by it.
