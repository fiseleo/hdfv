The distillation column's screen sets its throughput and its cut points (more diesel, or more naphtha), the vacuum section, and which product each outlet takes. Process units on its products make more of what you need.

{items hdfv:distillation_column hdfv:process_unit hdfv:unit_outlet hdfv:pump_jack}
- FCC: gasoline, diesel or olefins by its riser temperature. Reformer: high-octane gasoline and hydrogen. Steam cracker: ethylene and propylene.
- Polymer plant: plastics and synthetic rubber; methanol from steam reforming and synthesis.

A process unit is three reactor blocks of one kind over its panel, outlets either side; JEI's refining pages show every yield.
