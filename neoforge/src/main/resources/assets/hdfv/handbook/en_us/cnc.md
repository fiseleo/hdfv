The CNC machines to the finest grades and is the only machine for complex shapes: turbine blades, injector orifices, cooling holes. It runs unattended on electricity, and hoppers can feed it.

{image cnc}

It is a multiblock 4 wide, 3 deep and 3 high round its cabinet. The work holding decides the axes: a plain bed is 3 axes, a rotary table 4, a trunnion 5, a turning spindle makes it a mill-turn; a sub-spindle and turrets take it up to 12. More axes mean fewer setups.
