A part is made like a standard component, only with more components: each one follows its own route through the machines (some may need the CNC). Bring stock of the materials the drawing names.

When every component is finished, put them on the {item hdfv:assembly_bench} with the drawing: it fits the {item hdfv:part} together (engines, gearboxes, axles, propellers). Bodies and wings are not fitted there: the assembly room builds them from components in place.
