High Degree of Freedom Vehicles is about building vehicles the way engineers do: you design every part yourself, make it in a workshop of real machine tools, and put the vehicle together in an assembly room. Its physics, from engines to tyres, wings and hulls, is AeroCore's.

## The road ahead
- Mine the new ores and smelt them; make coke and steel in the alloy furnace.
- Cast and forge the first standard components; fit the first machine tools together on the {item hdfv:assembly_bench}.
- Power the workshop: a treadle, then steam, line shafts and electricity.
- Design an engine, machine its parts, run it on an engine stand.
- Build the car in a car factory, fuel it and drive it. Aircraft, boats, trucks and the rest branch off from there.

## Finding your way

Every hdfv item says in its tooltip which tech stage it belongs to and where it is made. The advancements screen ({key key.advancements}) has a High Degree of Freedom Vehicles tab that follows this handbook: an entry here gets a tick once its advancement is done.
