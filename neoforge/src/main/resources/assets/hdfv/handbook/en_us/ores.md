Eight new ores lie in stone and deepslate: bauxite, ilmenite, nickel, chromite, magnesite, tin, zinc and garnet. Nickel, tin and zinc smelt in a furnace; the others are worked later, most of them in the electric furnaces.

## The alloy furnace

The {item hdfv:alloy_furnace} is the first hdfv machine: bricks, a blast furnace and iron at the crafting table. It burns fuel; first make {item hdfv:coke} from coal, then steel from iron and coke.

## Stock

What comes out is {item hdfv:stock}: the metal the workshop cuts, one item per material (steel, bronze, grey iron, aluminium...). An ingot of it is 10 kg; a {item hdfv:stock_bit} is a tenth. Machines take the stock their drawing needs and give chips back as stock bits.
