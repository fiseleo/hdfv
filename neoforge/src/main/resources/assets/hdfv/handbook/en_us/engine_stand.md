Put a finished engine in an {item hdfv:engine_stand} and pour fuel in from a can. Use it with an empty hand to start or stop it; use it sneaking to change the governor's speed. A running engine drives the line shaft it touches, so it can power your workshop.

{image engine_stand}

{recipe hdfv:engine_stand}

It burns what the engine map says at that load: this is the same engine your vehicle will carry.
