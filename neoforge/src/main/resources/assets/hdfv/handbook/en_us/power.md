Machine tools need power. The workshop starts with a **line shaft**: blocks of {item hdfv:line_shaft} in a row, turning at 250 rpm. Whatever touches the line drives it or takes from it.

{items hdfv:treadle hdfv:line_shaft hdfv:pulley_drive hdfv:steam_engine}
- A {item hdfv:treadle}: stand on it and you pedal about 75 W into the shaft. Slow, but it needs nothing.
- A {item hdfv:steam_engine}: 15 kW from coal, charcoal, coke or wood. This is how a workshop really starts.
- A machine tool takes the shaft's power through a {item hdfv:pulley_drive} touching the line. Too much load slows the shaft and every machine on it.
