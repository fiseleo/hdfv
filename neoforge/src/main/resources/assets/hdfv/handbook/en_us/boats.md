Boats are built in a **shipyard** ({item hdfv:shipyard_controller}): a {item hdfv:slipway} on the floor, its door onto water. A hull is a body of modules; displacement or planing, the sea and AeroCore's waves decide how it rides.

{design runabout_hull}

{key key.forward} and {key key.back} are the throttle, {key key.left} and {key key.right} the rudder.
