Beyond piston engines: turbojets, turbofans, turboprops and turboshafts, afterburners ({key key.hdfv.reheat}), ramjets and scramjets that only work fast, pulsejets that buzz, and solid rocket boosters ({key key.hdfv.fire}) you reload with propellant grains.

{design j79}

Hydrogen comes as 700 bar gas in cylinders or as a liquid that boils off unless kept cold. Very fast vehicles heat up and need the world ahead loaded: AeroCore takes care of that.
