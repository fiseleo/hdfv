Grade B components (turned shafts, leadscrews, tubes, slideways) come from the lathe, the drill press and the planer. With them you fit the milling machine and the boring mill; with those the grinders, the gear and thread machines: grade C (cut gears, bearings, spindles, ball screws, circuit boards).

{items hdfv:milling_machine hdfv:grinder hdfv:cut_gear hdfv:welding_station}

There are nine families of machining: grinding, finishing, gears, shaping and broaching, sawing, electrical machining, cutting tables, threading and riveting. Each is a multiblock whose head decides what it is. Many can take an NC drive and then run without you.

The {item hdfv:welding_station} welds formed sheet; a player or a welder villager works it.
