Machines are not crafted whole: they are fitted together from **standard components** on the {item hdfv:assembly_bench}. The first components are grade A, castings and forgings that only the {item hdfv:foundry} and the {item hdfv:forging_press} make. Those three are made at the crafting table.

## Making a standard component
- Open the design screen with a {item hdfv:design_table} or a {item hdfv:design_tablet}; in the Standard components group pick a kind (a bed casting, a forged shaft...) and save it onto a {item hdfv:blank_blueprint}.
- Put the drawing in the machine's drawing slot and stock in its inputs. Forming machines work by themselves; the arrows choose which component they start.
- Grades B, C and D come later, from the lathe, the drill press and the planer, then the mills and grinders, then the CNC.

## Fitting a machine

Put every component a machine needs on the {item hdfv:assembly_bench} and keep its screen open: fitting takes hours of work. JEI's assembly bench page lists them.
