Aircraft are built in a **hangar** ({item hdfv:hangar_controller}), its door onto a clear apron. Wings, propellers and rotors are designed like everything else: an airfoil and a planform become lift and drag.

- {key key.hdfv.flaps} flaps, {key key.hdfv.landing_gear} the landing gear, {key key.hdfv.brake} the wheel brakes, {key key.hdfv.air_start} an air start for a turbine.
