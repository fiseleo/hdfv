Airports generate in the world: large, medium and small, with runways, lights and a tower. You can build your own: a {item hdfv:control_tower} is a multiblock you can build taller; on electricity it runs radar, ATIS, the runway lights and clearances.

{image tower}

In an aircraft: {key key.hdfv.atc_call} calls the tower, {key key.hdfv.atc_atis} asks for the ATIS, {key key.hdfv.atc_cancel} cancels. A controller villager works the tower, or a player takes over at its desk. Flying without a clearance is only warned, never stopped.

{see aircraft}
