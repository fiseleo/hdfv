A refit shop is a room like an assembly room with a {item hdfv:refit_shop_controller}. Drive a finished car in and change it: its shape, its size (the whole car or each part of it), an aero kit, rims and sidewall lettering, tuning, and lamps placed anywhere.

The shop checks the car again, fits the new parts from its chests (each lamp needs a {item hdfv:lamp_unit}), and swaps the car where it stands, keeping its fuel. What comes off goes back.
