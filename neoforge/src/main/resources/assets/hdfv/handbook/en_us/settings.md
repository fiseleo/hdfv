Every sound is synthesized from the machine or the engine as it runs: there are no sound files. Its volumes are in the mod's settings (the Mods list, then Config): vehicles count as neutral creatures, machines as blocks.

## Server options
- kgPerIngot (10) and ticksPerWorkHour (1000): how much metal an ingot is and how long an hour of work takes.
- consumables, chipRecovery: whether tools wear out, and how much of the cut metal comes back.
- damage: whether vehicles are damaged and how hard each cause counts; storage.itemMassScale: what items weigh.
- Stickers, airports and this tutorial (giveHandbook, welcome) can be turned off.
