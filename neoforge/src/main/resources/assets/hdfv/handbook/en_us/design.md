Open the design screen with the {item hdfv:design_table} or the {item hdfv:design_tablet}. Parts are grouped by kind (engines, airframes, bodies, chassis, wheels, drivetrain, standard components...). Start from a sample or a new design.

{design inline_four}
- A piston engine is sixteen components: block, head, pistons, rods, crank, valves, cam, plugs, injectors, valve train, fuel, ignition, lubrication, cooling, intake and exhaust. Every number is yours.
- Red issues must be fixed; yellow ones are warnings (outside the tested range, for instance). The overview shows the result in 3D.
- The bench page runs your engine on a dynamometer: power and torque curves, fuel use. Change something and run it again.
- Save writes the design onto a blueprint (a blank one is used up). That blueprint is the drawing the workshop makes it from.

An engine for your first car: a small petrol inline four or a diesel. Mind the fuel it is designed for: naphtha from the first refinery is only 70 octane; a diesel burns diesel and kerosene.
