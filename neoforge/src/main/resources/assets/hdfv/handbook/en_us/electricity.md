A {item hdfv:generator} on the line shaft turns shaft power into Forge Energy (50 kW); {item hdfv:cable} carries it to machines, a {item hdfv:battery} stores it, an {item hdfv:electric_motor} turns another line. Machines with a {item hdfv:motor_drive} take it directly.

{items hdfv:generator hdfv:cable hdfv:battery hdfv:electric_motor hdfv:motor_drive}

## Electric smelting

The {item hdfv:arc_furnace} makes alloy and stainless steel, ferro-alloys, superalloys and titanium; the {item hdfv:electrolysis_cell} wins aluminium and magnesium. Each batch costs the kWh its chemistry needs.
