A car is built in a **car factory**: a closed room with {item hdfv:assembly_floor} underfoot, a door ({item hdfv:roller_shutter}) at floor level, and the {item hdfv:car_factory_controller} set in a wall, its front inside. A {item hdfv:crane_rail} in the roof lifts heavy parts. Eight by five metres and four high is a good start.

{image car_factory}

{items hdfv:car_factory_controller hdfv:assembly_floor hdfv:roller_shutter hdfv:crane_rail hdfv:welding_station}
- Scan: the controller checks the room and says what is missing.
- Layout: put the part blueprints (body, chassis, axles, engine, gearbox...) in its drawing slots and place them in the layout; it checks that they fit (an engine must fit its bay) and that the car makes sense. A {item hdfv:vehicle_blueprint} keeps a layout.
- Start: it builds from the parts, components and stock in chests inside the room. Welding stations in the room, worked by players or welders, are its crew.
- Launch: when it is done, the door opens and the car rolls out.
