Trucks start from a ladder frame chassis; a cab-over body sits on it. A tractor carries a fifth wheel, a semi-trailer a kingpin and landing legs. Back the tractor under the trailer and press {key key.hdfv.couple} to couple; press it again to lower the legs and leave it.
