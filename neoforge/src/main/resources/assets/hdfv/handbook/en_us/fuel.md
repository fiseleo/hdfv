Vehicles leave the assembly room with empty tanks, and their fuels come from oil. Look for {item hdfv:oil_seep} on the surface, then use the {item hdfv:geophone} on the ground: it tells whether a field lies below, how deep, whether it flows and where its middle is.
- The drilling rig drills down and sets a {item hdfv:wellhead} when it strikes oil; while the field has pressure it flows by itself, later a pump jack lifts it.
- Pipe the crude to the crude distillation column: naphtha, kerosene, diesel and fuel oil come out at its outlets.
- Fill a {item hdfv:jerry_can} (20 l) and use it on the vehicle, or build a {item hdfv:fuel_dispenser}.
