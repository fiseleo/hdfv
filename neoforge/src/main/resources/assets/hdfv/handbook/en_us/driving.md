Fuel the car first (it leaves the factory empty), then get in. {key key.forward} and {key key.back} are throttle and brake, {key key.left} and {key key.right} steer; {key key.sneak} gets out.

{image sedan}

- Gears: {key key.hdfv.shift_up} and {key key.hdfv.shift_down}; with a manual gearbox you work the clutch ({key key.hdfv.clutch}) yourself and can stall. {key key.hdfv.selector} moves an automatic's selector (P, R, N, D).
- {key key.hdfv.handbrake} is the handbrake, {key key.hdfv.drive_mode} the four-wheel-drive mode, {key key.hdfv.diff_lock} the diff lock, {key key.hdfv.ride_height} the ride height of air suspension.
- {key key.hdfv.lights} lights, {key key.hdfv.indicate_left} and {key key.hdfv.indicate_right} indicators, {key key.hdfv.horn} the horn, {key key.hdfv.wipers} the wipers, {key key.hdfv.doors} the doors.

The dashboard shows speed, revs, gear and fuel. All keys can be changed in the controls, under High Degree of Freedom Vehicles.

## Roads and ramps
{items hdfv:asphalt hdfv:asphalt_ramp_2 hdfv:asphalt_ramp_4 hdfv:asphalt_ramp_8 hdfv:apron_concrete hdfv:concrete_ramp_2 hdfv:concrete_ramp_4 hdfv:concrete_ramp_8}
Pave with {item hdfv:asphalt} (gravel and the refinery's bitumen) or {item hdfv:apron_concrete}. A ramp rises one block over 2, 4 or 8 blocks: 1:8 is a gentle road, 1:2 a steep one. Put the first piece down looking uphill and the next ones against it: each carries the slope on by itself, and a piece set a block higher beyond the top starts the next flight. Wheels drive up a ramp as a real slope.
